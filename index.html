Remedial Coaching & Mentoring

Our Remedial Coaching programs focus on identifying the root causes of a child’s learning difficulties and strengthening their academic foundation. We aim to rebuild confidence, improve understanding, and enhance overall learning ability & provide personalized one-to-one support for children facing challenges in areas such as language, mathematics, attention, and concentration. Based on the child’s needs, we offer short-term intensive coaching as well as long-term mentoring, ensuring steady progress and sustainable improvement.

Know More about Remedial Coaching

Psychological Counselling

Mental well-being is essential for a balanced life, and the right support can make a meaningful difference. We offer compassionate, confidential counselling for concerns such as stress, anxiety, family conflicts, adolescent issues, and addiction. Our services include individual, couple, family, career, and specialized addiction counselling, delivered by trained professionals in both Malayalam and English.

Know More about Psychological Counselling

Transformative Training Services

Our transformative workshops are designed to unlock potential across all age groups—students, parents, and teachers. Programs like VibeOn (for personality growth), the Instant Boosting Program (for goal setting), and specialized workshops on parenting, motivation, and emotional intelligence provide practical skills for immediate, positive change and long-term development.

Know More about Training Programs

Diet Consulting

Good health is the foundation of a happy and focused life. Our certified nutrition experts provide personalized, practical diet plans and wellness support for children and families. Whether addressing deficiencies that affect focus or guiding overall family health, our online and offline consulting ensures holistic physical well-being.

FAQs

What are the services of De Beacon?

De Beacon is a holistic center for Remedial Coaching, Psychological Counseling, Training, and Mentoring.

What is the speciality of remedial classes by De Beacon?

De beacon offers personalized one-on-one remedial classes for students from KG to Grade 10 as a key service.

Who are the key beneficiaries of de beacon’s services?

Students, adolescents, and families who are looking for support in overcoming academic, emotional, and developmental challenges through professional support.

Who are the team behind de beacon?

We have a team of committed psychologists, remedial experts, mentors, Trainers, and social workers.

What is VibeOn program?

VibeOn is Designed to create the stars of tomorrow . VibeOn focuses on life skills that go beyond the regular school syllabus—because the future needs more than just grades. Through interactive programs, workshops, training sessions, and mentoring experiences, students develop emotional intelligence, communication skills, creativity, leadership, decision-making, and resilience.

Who can join VibeOn?

Students from 3 rd to 9 std (English and Malayalam medium).

What about the duration of VibeOn?

One full day training program in every month for a year.

Can student continue in VibeOn after 1 year?

Yes, there will be advanced VibeOn for those who complete one year.

Why VibeOn?

Rooted in the concept of multiple intelligences, VibeOn nurtures every child’s unique strengths—academic, emotional, social, creative, and practical—ensuring total development of the student, not just classroom performance. It’s about helping young minds discover their vibe, own their potential, and step confidently into the future.
