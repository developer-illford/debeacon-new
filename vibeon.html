VibeOn by De Beacon: Turn on the Vibe of Growth in Your Child

VibeOn is a specialized long term training program designed to create the “stars of tomorrow “. VibeOn focuses on life skills that go beyond the regular school syllabus—because the future needs more than just grades. Through interactive programs, workshops, training sessions, and mentoring experiences, students develop emotional intelligence, communication skills, creativity, leadership, decision-making, and resilience.

Why VibeOn?

Rooted in the concept of multiple intelligences, VibeOn nurtures every child’s unique strengths—academic, emotional, social, creative, and practical—ensuring total development of the student, not just classroom performance. It’s about helping young minds discover their vibe and potential, and step confidently into the future

Program Structure

VibeOn is divided into two cohorts to ensure age-appropriate activities and focused outcomes:

Why VibeOn is Different?

  • Joyful Exploration: We use interactive games, real-life scenarios, and engaging discussions instead of lectures.

  • Expert Facilitators: Sessions are led by experienced psychologists and certified trainers who understand child development.

  • Holistic Growth: Focused equally on academic readiness and socio-emotional maturity.
