Who We Are

De Beacon – Lighting the Path to Growth and Happiness

De Beacon stands for light, hope, and guidance—and that is exactly what we offer to every learner and family who comes to us. We are a dedicated support and development center helping children, adolescents, and individuals grow academically, emotionally, and personally.

By combining one-to-one remedial coaching, mentoring, counselling, and guided learning, we ensure each person receives the support they need to move forward. We focus on strengthening learning skills, building confidence, improving communication, enhancing emotional balance, and creating a positive mindset. Our work contributes to society by helping children learn better, supporting families through challenges, and promoting healthier emotional well-being.

The Story of De Beacon

In a town where friendship and shared purpose run deep, three friends — Dr. Mohamed Anas, Mr.Kunjoy and Mr. Jamal shareef — nurtured a dream for over a decade : to create opportunities for every student and community to thrive. For decades, they had been working with different social and educational organizations, dedicating themselves to the upliftment of marginalized students and communities. Kunjoyi served as District Coordinator for Childline 1098, supporting and guiding children in vulnerable situations. Dr. Anas devoted himself to extensive community work, ensuring opportunities reached those who needed them most. Jamal, an Engineer by profession and passionate about human potential development, applied over 20 years of voluntary training experience and techniques like Transactional Analysis to help students unlock their strengths and confidence. Their combined experiences are spanned across organizations such as MECT (Manipuram Education and Charitable Trust), CIGI (Centre for Information and Guidance, India), and FACE — which runs FACE Public School in Calicut, focusing on holistic development and skill-based education.

Their vision had been discussed for many years, but it began taking shape in the early months of 2025. Sitting together in long, thoughtful conversations, they realized that their shared passion and complementary experiences could be combined to create something unique — a place where students from all backgrounds, especially marginalized communities, could receive specialized training, mentorship, counselling and guidance. These discussions planted the seed that would grow into De Beacon, a center committed to nurturing the ‘Stars of Tomorrow.’

De Beacon provide more than what schools typically offer — specialized skills, creative training, cognitive development, and programs that nurture multiple intelligences. Our mission is to empower students with holistic education, practical skills, and the confidence to explore and realize their true potential.

At De Beacon, the mission is simple yet powerful: to shape well-rounded, confident, and capable individuals who are ready to make a meaningful impact on the world. Here, every student is seen, heard, and guided — a journey toward becoming the ‘Stars of Tomorrow.’”

Vision & Mission

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To be a beacon of hope and growth — empowering individuals everywhere to overcome barriers, nurture resilience, and achieve emotional, academic, and personal well-being through compassionate guidance and transformative support.

Our Promise

Whether you walk into our center or connect with us online, De Beacon is here to guide you towards brighter learning, stronger living, and greater well-being.

Our Mission

At De Beacon, we unite remedial coaching, mentoring, psychological counseling, transformative training, and diet consulting into a holistic service — accessible both in-person and online. Our mission is to:

  1. Make expert guidance available to anyone, anywhere, breaking geographical barriers.
  2. Personalize support plans that address unique academic, emotional, and lifestyle needs.
  3. Blend science-backed strategies with empathetic care to spark lasting transformation.
  4. Foster resilience, confidence, and lifelong skills that extend beyond classrooms and clinics.
  5. Create a trusted, confidential, and approachable space where progress is celebrated and potential is realized.
