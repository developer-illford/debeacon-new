Ms. Anagha K

Psychologist | Consultant Psychologist

Ms. Anagha K is an experienced Consultant Psychologist with a strong background in neurodevelopmental disorders, counselling, and psychological assessments. She is skilled in multidisciplinary collaboration and delivering therapeutic interventions that support individual growth and development.

Phone: 7909212811
Location: Koduvally, Kozhikode
Specialization: Neurodevelopmental Disorders & Counselling

Career Milestones

  • Expertise in psychological assessment, diagnosis, treatment planning, and behavioral therapy.
  • Experienced in handling cases of Autism Spectrum Disorder, ADHD, SLD, GDD, IDD, and behavioral issues.
  • Strong background in adolescent counselling, individual therapy, and patient progress monitoring.
  • Skilled in developing Individualized Education Plans (IEP) and conducting workshops.
  • Proven ability to collaborate with healthcare professionals for comprehensive interventions.

Educational Qualification

  • M.Sc. Applied Psychology — University of Calicut (2019–2021)
  • Diploma in Specific Learning Disorder — Mentex School of Counselling and Psychotherapy (2024–2025)

  • SSLC — Govt. Higher Secondary School, Koduvally

Professional Profile

Ms. Anagha has served as a Psychologist at the Decibel Institute of Hearing Balance and Speech Science, Thodupuzha (Dec 2022 – Mar 2025), where she conducted assessments, diagnosis, therapy planning, documentation, IEP development, and parental psychoeducation.

She has also worked at Sukrutham Rehab Institution (May 2022 – Nov 2022), delivering therapy for children with developmental disorders, documenting case histories, and collaborating with multidisciplinary teams.

Her internship experience at Human Care Foundation, Calicut, further strengthened her skills in assessment, behavioral observation, treatment planning, and coordinated therapeutic interventions.

Certifications & Training

  • Behavior Support for Toddlers – AFIRM Autism
