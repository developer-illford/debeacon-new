Compassionate Psychological Counselling for Mental Wellness

Mental health matters — with the right support, peace and balance are within reach.

At De Beacon, we provide a safe and confidential space for individuals, couples, and families to navigate life’s challenges. Through compassionate listening, evidence-based assessment, and practical guidance, we help you rebuild emotional balance, strengthen confidence, and move toward lasting well-being.

Our Specialized Counselling Services

We offer a range of specialized, session-based support tailored to your unique needs, provided by experienced psychologists in Malayalam and English.

  • Counselling for Students and Adolescents
  • Individual / Personal Counselling
  • Marriage / Couple Counselling
  • Family Counselling
  • Substances and Screen Addiction Counselling

Counselling for Students and Adolescents

This service is specifically designed for school and college-aged individuals navigating academic stress, peer pressure, exam anxiety, and the challenges of identity formation. We focus on improving concentration, boosting self-esteem, managing mood swings, and developing essential coping mechanisms for a resilient future.

വ്യക്തിഗത കൗൺസിലിംഗ് (Individual / Personal Counselling)

Confidential sessions for adults dealing with personal mental health concerns such as chronic stress, anxiety disorders, depression, unresolved trauma, or difficulties with anger management and self-esteem. We provide one-to-one guidance to process emotional pain and equip you with customized strategies for personal growth and emotional mastery.

Book Individual Counselling Now

ദാമ്പത്യ കൗൺസിലിംഗ് (Marriage / Couple Counselling)

Support dedicated to strengthening relationships. Our couple counselling helps partners improve communication, resolve recurring conflicts, navigate major life transitions (like parenting or career changes), and rebuild mutual respect and emotional intimacy to ensure a harmonious partnership

Book Couple Counselling Now

കുടുംബ കൗൺസിലിംഗ് (Family Counselling)

Focused on improving the overall health and functionality of the family unit. We work with all family members to address inter-generational conflicts, behavioral issues in children, communication breakdown, and to establish healthy boundaries and a supportive, peaceful home environment.

Book Family Counselling Now

Substances and Screen Addiction Counselling

Specialized support for those struggling with dependency on technology (gaming, social media, screen time) or other substances. We use targeted therapeutic approaches to understand the underlying causes of addiction, develop healthy coping mechanisms, and restore balance and control over one's life.

Book Addiction Counselling Now
