Ms. Ayisha Beevi K. C.

Remedial Expert | Specialist in Learning Disability Management – De Beacon

Ms. Ayisha Beevi K. C. is a committed Remedial Expert who believes that every child deserves the opportunity to learn at their own pace and discover confidence through progress. With over six years of experience guiding children with learning challenges, she specializes in helping students overcome reading, writing, and comprehension difficulties through structured, compassionate intervention.

Experience: 6+ Years
Position: Remedial Expert

  • Specialized in Learning Disability (LD) Management.

Professional Profile

Ms. Ayisha Beevi K. C. is a dedicated educator whose mission is to help every child discover their unique learning rhythm. Her background in Nursing and Early Childhood Education enables her to approach each learner holistically—addressing emotional, cognitive, and behavioral dimensions together.

At De Beacon, she plays a pivotal role in the Remedial Coaching & Mentoring Department, creating individualized learning plans, fostering parent-teacher collaboration, and ensuring that each session is both skill-oriented and confidence-building.

Core Expertise

  • Learning Disability Assessment & Intervention
  • Language and Phonetic Development
  • Reading & Writing Skill Enhancement
  • Individualized Remedial Coaching Plans
  • Behavioral and Motivational Support

Vision

“To create a learning environment where every child—regardless of their challenges—feels understood, supported, and inspired to reach their fullest potential. My goal is to make learning a joyful and empowering journey that builds confidence for life.”

At De Beacon, we understand that life's challenges can sometimes feel overwhelming, and seeking support can be a daunting step. That's why our team of dedicated professionals is here to provide you with the compassionate guidance and expert care you deserve.
