Mr. Jamal Shareef

Co-founder & Director – De Beacon | Engineer | HR Professional | Human Potential Trainer

Mr. Jamal Shareef is an engineer by qualification and a people enthusiast by passion. Combining his background in Engineering with an MBA in Human Resource Management, he brings together technical precision and human understanding — the foundation of his work at De Beacon, a leading organization offering psychological counseling, remedial coaching, transformative training, and holistic diet consulting across Kerala and the GCC region.

Experience: 20+ Years
Position:Co-Founder & Director
Specialization: Human Development & Organizational Growth

Career Milestones

  • Started his professional career as a Lecturer in Electronics & Communication Engineering at KMCT Engineering College, Calicut.
  • Transitioned into the corporate sector with IBM India, Bangalore, gaining hands-on experience in technology and team development.
  • Currently based in the GCC, serving as an IT Specialist, blending innovation with effective teamwork in a multicultural setting.
  • Over two decades of experience in training and development, focusing on human potential and leadership growth.
  • Renowned for inspiring individuals and teams through transformative learning sessions across Kerala, India, and abroad.

Educational Background

  • Bachelor’s Degree in Electronics & Communication Engineering
  • Master of Business Administration (MBA) in Human Resource Management
  • Advanced Trainings in Transactional Analysis, Emotional Intelligence, and Life Skills Training

Professional Experience & Contributions

Mr. Jamal’s career spans education, technology, and personal development. He began as an educator, evolved through corporate and IT roles, and emerged as a trainer and social entrepreneur dedicated to human capability enhancement and emotional wellbeing.

  • Founder Director – De Beacon
  • IT Specialist – GCC Region (Current)
  • Lecturer – KMCT Engineering College, Calicut (Early Career)
  • Corporate Professional – IBM India, Bangalore

Training & Development Expertise

  • Specializes in Human Capability Enhancement, Emotional Intelligence, and Life Skills Training.
  • Conducted leadership, motivational, and personality development sessions across Kerala and the GCC.
  • Trainer in Transactional Analysis (TA) and experiential learning methodologies.

Community & Organizational Roles

  • Founder Director – De Beacon, fostering holistic education and mental wellness programs.
  • Executive Trustee – FACE Charitable Trust, Calicut.
  • Contributor to the establishment of FACE Public School (ALDI syllabus).
  • Active association with CIGI, MECT, and ITPN Qatar for over two decades, promoting educational and social initiatives.

Vision

“Every individual is born with the capability to excel — all it takes is a spark of guidance to help them realize their potential. Through De Beacon, my mission is to ignite that spark and guide people toward balanced growth in mind, body, and spirit.”

At De Beacon, we understand that life's challenges can sometimes feel overwhelming, and seeking support can be a daunting step. That's why our team of dedicated professionals is here to provide you with the compassionate guidance and expert care you deserve.
